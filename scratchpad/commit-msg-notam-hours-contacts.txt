NOTAM activity hours + upcoming NOTAMs; labeled numbers per coordination authority

NOTAMs:
- The feed's item D) schedule ("DAILY 0500-1500", UTC) is parsed from the raw
  text and shown as Israel-time hours (DST-aware) in the map card banner/
  details, the NOTAM popup, and anywhere the NOTAM is listed.
- The feed now carries NOTAMs that have not started yet (it used to drop
  everything not in force at fetch time). The map, HUD and point inspector
  still only treat what is active right now as active; the card lists
  upcoming ones separately. A request is judged on its own flight window:
  the request form, the dispatcher view and the server action all use
  checkLiveNotamOverlap(point, notams, window), so a NOTAM that starts
  tomorrow no longer lets a request for tomorrow auto-clear.

Coordination contacts (0091):
The previous change replaced the tower's phone number with a WhatsApp
button in the contacts list, hiding it. Authorities now have an ordered
list of labeled numbers (contacts jsonb) — e.g. Hatzor: "מגדל פיקוח"
073-3485024 and "תיאום טיסות" 058-672-6900 — each shown with its number
and, next to it, a WhatsApp button (mobile) or a call button (landline).
Legacy phone/backup_phone/whatsapp_phone columns are kept in sync and used
as a fallback for rows without a list. The edit form is a labeled-numbers
editor.
