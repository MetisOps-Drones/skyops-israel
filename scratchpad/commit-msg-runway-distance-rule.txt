Measure the 2 km rule from the runway, not the bubble or the CTR boundary

Corrects the previous zone-enforcement pass, which got the rule wrong:
- It measured the 2 km from the CTR/ATZ boundary and from the drawn
  bubble's edge, and made everything inside a CTR forbidden.
- The regulation measures from the aerodrome/base RUNWAY, and the intended
  outcome is: within 2 km of the runway = forbidden; inside the CTR/ATZ
  beyond 2 km = flyable with the tower's coordination; outside = no zone
  restriction. Always judged on the requested point, never on the bubble.

Changes:
- aerodrome-runways.ts: runway centrelines of every Israeli aerodrome and
  air force base (OpenStreetMap aeroway=runway, 69 runways; model-aircraft
  fields and non-Israeli airfields excluded). Static, so the check stays
  local and instant and the server action uses identical data.
- flight-rules.ts: nearestRunway() + AERODROME_FORBIDDEN_RADIUS_KM; only
  PROHIBITED and the runway distance are "forbidden"; CTR/ATZ/TMA/CTA are
  "coordination with the tower"; footprint/radius parameters removed.
- aip.ts: only PROHIBITED zeroes the ceiling from the ground; CTR/ATZ no
  longer do (a whole control zone read as "אסור" even 10 km out).
- 0088 rewritten: no longer imports the air-force-base restriction discs
  (5-10 km, they would forbid the coordination band); keeps only the 6
  missing official prohibited areas. Because it deletes by source tag it
  also removes the 8 base discs if the first version was already run.
- Dispatcher-facing zone text and the map card/request form wording
  updated to match.

Checked against production zone data: a point 4 km from the Hatzor runway
and the Shapir settlement (3.1 km from the nearest runway) resolve to
"coordination"; points under 2 km from the runway resolve to forbidden.
