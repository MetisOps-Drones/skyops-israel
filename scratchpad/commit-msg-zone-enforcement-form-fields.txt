Enforce zone types and airport/base distances; request-form fields; radius crash fix; WhatsApp link; rename to Metisim

Zone enforcement (flight-rules.ts, aip.ts, flight-requests.ts, both pilot cards):
The AIP/regulation text the app is built on (the hobby and commercial
study material) says "לא ניתן להטיס באזור פיקוח, באזור פיקוח שדה או באזור
שדה", within 2 km of an aerodrome runway, and "אין להטיס באזור אסור". The
code treated CTR/ATZ as a warning with a submit button, only gave the
2 km buffer a "coordinate it" outcome, and let any GND-floor zone of ANY
kind (including restricted) read as a flat "אסור" because of one shared
ground-block rule. Now each type has its own outcome:
  - CTR / ATZ / PROHIBITED (incl. the CAAI air-force-base restriction
    polygons) -> forbidden for every account, no submit path, also within
    2 km of a CTR/ATZ boundary, and judged on the bubble's footprint, not
    just its centre.
  - DANGER -> blocked solo, an org may submit while the dispatcher chases
    the CAAI director's sign-off (the previously unreachable org path).
  - RESTRICTED -> coordination request subject to the area's conditions.
  - TMA/CTA -> warning only (floors are far above a drone).
One zoneVerdictFor() owns the wording so the map card, the request form
and the server action no longer drift. The forbidden verdict is decided
from the local airspace layer alone and shown immediately, instead of
waiting on the slow OSM/building checks -- which is also why a point near
Hatzor could read as "special authorization needed" for several seconds.
The server action re-checks it and rejects.

Data (0088): the official CAAI drone map KMZ was compared layer by layer
against production. Its 48 "הגבלות בסיסי חיל אוויר" polygons left real
gaps outside the CTR/ATZ rows (east of Hatzor, north of Palmachim, Tel
Nof, Ovda); 6 of its 35 prohibited polygons had no counterpart. Added as
12+2 PROHIBITED rows, dissolved per base. Idempotent.

Radius crash: clearing the radius field pushed 0 into the store, and
buildFlightFootprint threw "Either radiusMeters or polygon must be
provided" out of a render. New BoundedNumberInput keeps a local draft and
only commits in-range numbers; the footprint builder no longer throws on a
transient empty radius. Regression test added.

Request form (0089): camera type (vertical / horizontal / aerial survey /
none) and pilot-declared takedown response time; flight altitude is now
entered in meters (it is stored as max_altitude_meters as before) with a
live feet / feet-AMSL (מעפ"י) readout, and the coordinator sees the same
conversion (ground elevation from the existing altitude API) in the
detail panel and in the forwarded message.

Coordinator (0090): coordination_authorities.whatsapp_phone, shown as a
link that opens the WhatsApp chat; Hatzor tower row set to 0586726900.

Rename: user-visible app name MetisOps -> Metisim (title, login/signup,
PDF report, SMS sender fallback, embeds, share footer, menu label, CSV
names). Left alone: component/identifier names, storage keys, the
metis-ops.com domain/User-Agent, and the Academy copy that points at the
