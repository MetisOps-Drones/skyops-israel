Map top bar and request form follow the location card

UI audit follow-ups:
- The top bar showed a red "forbidden" for anything that wasn't "none",
  including a control zone that only needs the tower's coordination (now the
  whole Gush Dan after 0094). It now reads the same verdict as the location
  card (zoneVerdictFor, with a new shortLabel), judged for the account's
  licence type and ground elevation, and no longer prints "up to 0 m".
- The bar started at the map's left edge and covered the zoom buttons; it now
  starts to their right.
- The request form leads with the verdict on the point instead of showing it
  below the drone-registration card, and the legacy airspace-status (demo)
  block that contradicted it is gone.

Tests: location-status.spec.ts (pure), map-hud-and-drawer.spec.ts (browser).
