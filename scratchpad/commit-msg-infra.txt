Sport/leisure pilot near infrastructure: a declaration, not a wall

Per the hobby regulation (הפעלת טיסן): within 150 m of infrastructure the
pilot may fly if the owner agreed (or it is his own), and a micro drone
(250 g or less) may fly over infrastructure under the regulation's
conditions. The app wrongly told every private account "you cannot
coordinate here" (the commercial regulation's special authorization).

- lib/geo/infrastructure-rule.ts: the decision, pure and tested.
- Request form: a hobby pilot near a building ticks a declaration (owner
  consent; or, for a micro drone, the micro-drone conditions). The submit
  button waits for it. Only the micro-drone declaration can be approved at
  once; owner consent goes to a dispatcher, never auto-approved.
- Server action: re-checks it (drone weight read from the drones table),
  rejects a missing/invalid declaration, stores it
  (flight_requests.infrastructure_declaration, migration 0096 - only written
  when used, so the app works before the migration is run).
- Dispatcher view shows the declaration; the edit dialog keeps it.
- Location card: no more "private account cannot coordinate"; says what is
  allowed and that a declaration is asked for.

Also: playwright workers pinned to 2 (the UI specs share one dev server and
one account), docs/HANDOFF.md for continuing on another machine, and the
zone-data generator scripts under scripts/zone-data.
