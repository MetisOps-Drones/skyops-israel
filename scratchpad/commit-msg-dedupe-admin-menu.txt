Drop the redundant "תיאומים" bubble for dispatcher_admin

Ohad's question: why do "תיאומים" and "מוקד תיאום" lead to the same
place? They don't share a route, but for dispatcher_admin, /dashboard
(the "תיאומים" target) renders nothing but a single link card pointing
straight at /ops -- everything else on that page is pilot-personal data
(own drones, license, flight hours) an admin account never has. So the
bubble was a dead-end detour to the exact same destination the "ops"
bubble already goes to directly.

Made "coordination" conditional on !isAdmin, mirroring how "ops" and
"admin" are already conditional on isAdmin. Pilots are unaffected --
/dashboard is a real, full personal dashboard for them, and there's no
"ops" bubble in their menu to duplicate against.
